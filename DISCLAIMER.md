# DISCLAIMER

HATWEAVE is an **experimental, theoretical research concept**.

- It has **not** been peer-reviewed.
- It has **not** undergone professional cryptanalysis.
- It must **never** be used to protect real data, real accounts, or real
  communications.
- Raw Sturmian sequences are low-complexity and predictable if their
  parameters are known — see Section 6 of [THESIS.md](THESIS.md) before
  forming any opinion about security strength.
- "Unbreakable" is the project's research aspiration, not a verified
  property of anything in this repository.

For production post-quantum cryptography, use the NIST standards:
ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), SLH-DSA (FIPS 205).

This project exists to explore an idea rigorously and in the open —
including publishing its weaknesses.
